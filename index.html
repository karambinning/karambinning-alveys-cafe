What we cook

From the pit
and the pot.

A Caribbean kitchen rooted in jerk, stews and good rice. We cook what's fresh that day — so the chalkboard moves. Pop in, or check Facebook for today's specials.

01 · Jerk

Smoke,
scotch bonnet,
allspice.

Marinated overnight, cooked the right way — chicken, sometimes pork, finished with our house jerk gravy. The thing we built the kitchen for.

02 · The pot

Slow stews:
curry goat,
oxtail, brown stew.

Pots on from morning. Curry goat with the bone in, oxtail with butter beans, brown-stew chicken when the mood says so. Ask what's on the hob today.

Rice & peas,

The proper sides. Rice & peas in coconut milk, fried plantain with a bit of caramel on the edge, fried dumplings hot off the pan, and coco bread for the patty.

Straight talk

We just opened.
So the menu still moves.

Alveys Café is brand new on Stockbrook Street — registered with Derby City Council and waiting on our first food hygiene inspection. We're keeping hours flexible while we settle in, and the daily menu depends on what we've got on the pit and the pot that morning.

Easiest way to catch us: send a message on Facebook before you head over. We'll let you know what's cooking and roughly when to come.

Heads up · opening times and prices aren't published yet — we'll add them to this page once we've settled into a steady rhythm. Until then, our Facebook page is the source of truth.
